MOM — HOW TO USE IT
====================

MOM lets students at VSSUT order food from campus stalls ahead of time, pay by UPI or
cash, and pick it up with a token and QR code. There are three kinds of people using it:

  1. Students            order food
  2. Stall owners/staff  run the stall: orders, pickup, menu, settings, sales
  3. MOM admin           adds stalls and their owners

Everyone signs in the same way: mobile number + a 6-digit code sent by SMS. No passwords.
The app works in any phone or laptop browser; on a phone you can "Add to Home Screen".


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open the app and tap "Sign in" (or open any page that needs you to be signed in).
  2. Type your 10-digit mobile number and tap "Send code".
  3. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS. (You can ask for a new code after 30 seconds.)
  4. First time only: type your name and tap "Continue".

You stay signed in on that device. "Sign out" is in the top bar.
Stall owners are added by the MOM admin first (see section 4); after that they simply
sign in with their number and land in their stall's console.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FOR STUDENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Find a stall
  - The home page lists the stalls: open ones first, with how busy they are
    ("Not busy, about 10 minute wait") and whether they take UPI and/or cash.
  - Search by stall name or by dish ("dosa").
  - Closed stalls show when they open next.

Order
  1. Open a stall, tap "Add" on dishes (use − / + to change the number).
     Sold-out dishes are greyed. Tap a dish's name or photo for details.
  2. Tap "View cart". Add a note for the stall if you like ("less spicy").
  3. Tap "Continue to payment" and choose:
       - Pay now by UPI       : subtotal + 1% platform fee.
       - Pay cash at counter  : subtotal + 1% platform fee + 5% cash-handling fee.
     A method the stall doesn't accept right now is greyed out ("Not accepted by this stall").
  4. Tap "Place order".
       - UPI: Razorpay opens; pay with your UPI app. You have 15 minutes, then the order expires.
       - Cash: you get your token immediately and pay at the counter.
  If a price changed or something sold out while you were ordering, the app shows the new
  bill instead of placing the order.

Your token
  - After ordering you see your token number (e.g. #23), a 4-digit pickup code and a QR code.
    The screen stays awake while it's open.
  - The status updates live: Waiting for the stall → Being prepared → READY (the screen turns
    green, your phone buzzes and plays a sound).
  - At the counter, show the QR code. If the scanner can't read it, tell them your token number
    and your 4-digit pickup code.

My orders ("Orders" tab)
  - Active orders and past orders, with live status.
  - Cancel: possible only before the stall starts preparing. UPI payments are refunded in full.
  - "Order again" refills your cart with the same dishes.
  - If a stall rejects your order you see their reason, and a UPI payment is refunded in full.

Cash rule: there's a limit on how many unpaid cash orders you can have open at once.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FOR STALL OWNERS AND COUNTER STAFF (the "stall console", /vendor)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Owners can use everything below. Counter staff can use only "Orders" and "Scan".
If you manage more than one stall, pick the stall in the top bar.

ORDERS — the live order board
  - Three columns: New → Preparing → Ready (on a phone they're tabs).
  - Tap "Turn on order sound" once when you open it: every new order then plays a chime,
    and the browser tab shows "(3)" for orders waiting to be accepted.
  - Each ticket shows the token, the dishes, the student's note, "UPI paid" or
    "Cash due ₹X", and how long it has waited (highlighted after 15 minutes).
  - Accept        → moves it to Preparing.
  - Mark ready    → the student is told at once. (Works straight from New for instant items.)
  - Reject        → choose a reason (the student sees it). A paid UPI order is refunded in full.
  - "Finished today" at the bottom lists collected, rejected and cancelled orders.

SCAN — handing food over
  - Hold the student's QR code in front of the camera (use the torch button in the dark).
  - No QR? Type the token number, tap Next, then the student's 4-digit pickup code.
  - You see a big result:
      ✅ Valid order   — token, dishes, note and payment. A warning appears if you haven't
                         marked it ready yet.
      ❌ with a reason — not a valid order / for another stall / already collected (with the
                         time) / not paid / cancelled or rejected.
  - Tap once to finish:
      UPI order:  "Hand over"
      Cash order: "Cash received ₹127.20 · Hand over"  (take the cash first!)
  - Then "Scan next".

MENU (owners)
  - Add categories (e.g. "Dosa", "Drinks"); rename, reorder with the arrows, delete when empty.
  - Add a dish: photo, name, price in ₹, veg / non-veg, description, available now.
    Photos are shrunk automatically before upload. Tap a dish to edit or delete it.
  - The switch on each dish row marks it available or "Sold out" instantly.
  - "Mark all available" brings everything back (e.g. each morning).
  - Every price change is recorded (who, old price, new price).

SETTINGS (owners)
  - Open or closed: "Follow hours", "Open now" or "Closed now". Open now / Closed now last
    until midnight, then the stall follows its hours again.
  - "Take new orders": switch off to pause when the kitchen is full; orders already placed
    carry on.
  - Payment methods: UPI and Cash switches. At least one must stay on. UPI can be turned on
    only once your settlement account is active ("Settlement account pending" until then).
  - Opening hours: several time slots per day, slots past midnight (6 PM – 1 AM) allowed,
    "Use for every day" copies a day to the whole week. Tap "Save hours".
  - Kitchen speed: minutes to prepare an order and how many orders you can cook at once.
    Students' waiting-time estimate uses these.
  - Stall details: photo, description, where to find you.

SALES (owners)
  - Today, Last 7 days or This month.
  - Sales (orders handed over), UPI and cash split, MOM's fees.
  - "Cash fees owed to MOM": with cash orders you keep the student's whole payment, so MOM's
    fees on those orders are owed back to MOM.
  - "UPI payouts": your UPI sales, paid to your bank by Razorpay.
  - A daily chart (hover or tap a bar for details, or "Show as a table") and
    "Download CSV" for Excel / Google Sheets.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FOR THE MOM ADMIN (/admin)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your number is made a super admin by the MOM team (it's listed in the server settings).
After signing in you land in the admin console.

STALLS — adding a stall and its owner
  1. "Stalls" → "Add stall": name, where it is, food types. It starts as a DRAFT
     (students can't see it).
  2. On the stall's page, under "Owner & staff", type the owner's mobile number, choose
     Owner (or Counter staff) and tap "Register". When they sign in with that number they
     go straight to this stall's console.
  3. Fill the go-live checklist ("Ready to go live: x of 5"):
       owner registered, at least one dish, opening hours, a stall photo, a payment method.
     "Edit menu" and "Hours, photo & payments" open that stall's console for you; whatever
     you change there is recorded under your name.
  4. Tap "Go live". If something is missing, the app tells you what.
  - "Suspend" hides a live stall from students at once (orders already placed carry on);
    "Make live again" / "Move to draft" undo it.
  - "Details" lets you change the name, web address, location, description, food types and
    veg-only flag.

UPI PAYOUTS for a stall
  - Under "UPI payouts" → "Set up": the owner's name, business name, email, mobile, address
    and bank account (account number, IFSC, account holder name) → "Send to Razorpay".
    Bank details go straight to Razorpay; MOM keeps only the last 4 digits.
  - Razorpay reviews the account. "Check status" asks Razorpay; once it says Active, the
    stall can switch UPI on in its settings.
  - If Razorpay refuses something (e.g. a wrong IFSC), fix it and send again: it continues
    where it stopped.
  - With Razorpay TEST keys there's "Mark active (test mode)" to try UPI without a real account.

USERS & ACCESS
  - Search anyone by name or phone; filter by role (student, stall owner, counter staff,
    super admin) or status. Open a person to see the stalls they manage, their recent
    orders and how many devices they're signed in on.
  - Suspend… (asks first): they're signed out everywhere — open pages stop within 15
    minutes — and can't sign in until you tap "Reactivate account".
  - Sign out everywhere: ends all their sessions (e.g. a lost phone).
  - Reset cash strikes: lets a student order with cash again.
  - Make / Remove super admin…: type the last 4 digits of their phone to confirm.
    MOM always keeps at least one active super admin, and numbers listed in the server
    settings stay super admin. A removed admin loses admin access at once.
  - Everything here is recorded in the audit log under your name.

ORDERS
  - Every order, newest first. Search by #token, phone number, student name or order id;
    filter by stall, status, payment and dates. "Show more" loads older ones.
  - Open an order to see the bill, the student and stall, Razorpay payments and refunds,
    and a timeline of what happened and who did it.
  - Cancel order… (with a reason): the student and the stall see it cancelled and the
    student sees your reason. A paid UPI order is refunded in full. If Razorpay can't be
    reached, the order is still cancelled and shows "Refund pending" with "Retry refund…".
  - Refund… (with a reason): money back for a finished UPI order (e.g. a complaint after
    pickup) without changing the order. Cash orders have no online payment to refund.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GOOD TO KNOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Light / dark mode: the screen icon in the top bar.
  - Prices: the fee lines are always shown separately on the bill.
  - Refunds for UPI orders go back to the student's UPI account (usually within a few days,
    as Razorpay processes them).
  - Problems at the counter? The manual token + pickup code always works without a camera.
